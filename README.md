# Nova Sales Analysis – Sales Performance, Profitability & Customer Insights

## 1. Project Overview
This project involves analyzing sales data for Nova Sales LTD, a fictional retail company, to evaluate business performance, profitability, product performance, and customer behaviour. The goal is to transform raw business data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 2. Business Context
Nova Sales LTD operates across multiple product categories and sales channels. Management requires a comprehensive understanding of its sales performance, profitability, customer purchasing patterns, and discount strategies to identify opportunities for business growth and operational improvement.

## 3. Project Objectives
- Evaluate revenue trends and overall gross profit performance across different periods.
- Identify top-performing sales channels, regions, countries, and product categories.
- Assess product profitability and the impact of discounts and shipping costs on business performance.
- Analyze customer segments, identify valuable customers, and evaluate repeat purchase behaviour.
- Evaluate order fulfilment performance, shipping timelines, cancellations, and product returns.
- Assess sales representative performance and provide actionable recommendations to improve business outcomes.
  
## 4. Business Questions
The analysis addresses the following 13 business questions
1. How is revenue changing over time?
2. Which sales channels generate the most revenue and gross profit?
3. Which regions and countries perform best?
4. Which product categories generate the most revenue and gross profit?
5. Which individual products are the most and least profitable?
6. Are discounts improving sales performance or reducing profitability?
7. How does shipping affect order economics?
8. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and profit?
9. Who are the most valuable customers?
10. Are customers returning to make repeat purchases?
11. How long does the company take to ship orders?
12. What proportion of orders are cancelled or returned?
13. Are sales representatives performing differently?
    
## 5. Dataset Description
The project uses four related datasets comprising Sales, Products, Customers, and Date tables, designed to support comprehensive sales and business performance analysis.

- **Sales Table:** Transactional and order-level information.
- **Products Table:** Product details and category information.
- **Customers Table:** Customer demographics and relevant customer attributes.
- **Date Table:** Calendar information used for time-based analysis, including monthly and yearly sales trends.

### Data Source
The datasets used in this project were generated with the assistance of ChatGPT specifically for the Nova Sales LTD portfolio project. The data is synthetic and designed to simulate realistic retail business transactions and operational activities for data analysis, business intelligence, and decision-making purposes.

## 11. Key Insights & Findings
The following figures are visible in the profitability dashboard:

- **Gross Profit:** 2.76M.
- **Profit Margin:** 77.59%.
- **Total Orders:** Approximately 4,490.
- **Order Cancellation Rate:** 6.23%.
- **Average Discount:** 9.79%.
- **Top Five Products by Profit:** LED Desk Lamp, Rice 5kg, Ballpoint Pens, Bluetooth Speaker, and Snack Box.
- **Bottom Five Products by Profit:** Tea Box, Desk Organizer, Laundry Basket, Denim Jeans, and Blender.

The dashboard also reveals differences in sales and profit contributions across product categories, sales channels, and discount bands.

## 12. Business Recommendations & Potential Impact
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can support management decisions:

- **Product Optimization:** Prioritize high-profit products and investigate the performance of low-profit products to improve product portfolio decisions.
- **Discount Management:** Review discount strategies to ensure promotional activities generate sustainable profitability.
- **Customer Retention:** Strengthen loyalty and retention initiatives to encourage repeat purchases.
- **Sales Channel Optimization:** Allocate resources towards channels delivering stronger sales and profit contributions.
- **Order Management:** Investigate cancellation patterns and identify opportunities to reduce cancelled orders.

**Potential Business Impact:**
Improved profitability, better inventory and product decisions, stronger customer retention, more effective promotional strategies, and increased operational efficiency.
